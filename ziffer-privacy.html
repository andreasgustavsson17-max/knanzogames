This policy explains what personal data is used in Ziffer, why it is needed and what rights you have. It also covers the Knanzo Games website and support. The initial launch is for Sweden and players aged 13 and over. Purchases and advertising have not yet been enabled for the public.

1. Responsibility and contact

The data controller is Andreas Carl Lars Gustavsson, a sole trader operating under the registered business name Knanzo Games. Contact us at support@knanzogames.se or at Knanzo Games, Plangatan 2, 441 70 Sollebrunn, Sweden.

You do not need to delete your account to ask questions or exercise your rights. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or payment card details to support.

2. Data we use

Account: we receive account identifiers and information provided by Apple or Google when you sign in, such as your email address. We store your username, avatar choices, progress, hints and account benefits. We do not receive the password to your Apple or Google account.

Registration: you provide your date of birth and country. Your new player profile stores the age-check result, age band (13–17 or 18+), country, check version and timestamp, not your full date of birth. For players aged 13–17, we also store their declaration of guardian permission. This information is self-declared, not verified age or guardian identity. We also record which terms and privacy information you received, the language and when you accepted the terms.

Gameplay: we process answers, scores, response times, statistics, matches, group participation, competition results and rewards. Friend relationships and invitations arise through your actions and those of other players.

Technical data and support: the services may process IP addresses, installation identifiers, notification tokens, technical logs and information used for app or device security checks. Settings and information needed to resume games are stored locally. For support, we process your message, contact details and any attachments.

3. What other players see

Other signed-in players can see your username, permitted avatar and gameplay-related points, even if they are not your friends. Match and group participants see relevant gameplay information. Leaderboards show participants' rankings and results.

Email addresses, notification tokens and private account and eligibility information are not included in the public profile. We do not display online status. Avoid including your address, school, phone number or other private information in your username.

4. Why we process data

Accounts, saved progress and the gameplay and social features you choose: necessary account and gameplay data is processed to perform our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Account-based online play cannot function without this data.

Eligibility, security and preventing cheating: necessary verification and technical data is processed on the basis of legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). The interest is to protect players and the functioning of the game. The same basis is used for requested game notifications and general support enquiries. Support needed to perform the gameplay contract is instead based on that contract. We must balance these interests against your privacy, particularly if you are a minor.

Data protection requests: data is used to fulfil our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Future processing requiring consent, such as certain advertising, will need separate consent (Article 6(1)(a)). Reading this policy or accepting the terms does not constitute that consent.

5. Providers and international transfers

Google/Firebase provides authentication, the database, server functions, notifications and verification of app requests. Apple and Google operate their sign-in and platform services. GitHub Pages hosts the website and Zoho Mail handles support email. Providers may also process service data for their own security and operational purposes. Data may be disclosed to authorities where required by law or used for legal claims.

Processing is not limited to Sweden or the EU/EEA. Firebase Authentication data is processed in the United States; other services use international infrastructure. Google's data protection terms describe transfer safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. These mechanisms apply within the scope of the relevant agreement, not automatically to every provider or transfer.

More information is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy, firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms, zoho.com/privacy.html and docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data and how to obtain a copy.

6. The website and notifications

Our web pages have no analytics tools, advertising cookies or contact forms, and load fonts from the website itself. GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Email links open your email application.

Notifications are used, for example, for friend requests, invitations and when it is your turn. You can turn them off in your phone's settings. This stops them being displayed but does not necessarily stop all technical communication from the notification service.

7. Planned advertising and purchases

Google AdMob is integrated, but advertising is disabled in the standard release configuration. The plan is to offer optional video ads for an extra hint and ads during breaks in gameplay. Before activation, the information and privacy choices must reflect the processing being introduced, including safeguards for younger players.

When advertising is activated, Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Even non-personalised ads may use identifiers or device storage. Required advertising consents and any Apple tracking permission are separate from the account terms and must be changeable.

Video rewards are prepared for server verification using session and transaction references, timestamps and reward status. Choosing a rewarded video is not general consent to tracking.

Purchases have not yet been enabled. If introduced, the App Store or Google Play will handle payment. We will then need purchase and subscription status to provide benefits, not full payment card details. This information will be updated before new processing begins.

8. Retention and account deletion

Your account, progress, avatar choices and statistics are stored for your account and games. Inactive accounts are not automatically deleted. Match history being hidden after 48 hours does not mean it has been deleted from the server.

Automatic cleanup is enabled for completed match details after 90 days, group rooms 48 hours after room expiry, and old video reward records after 7 days from the applicable expiry or reward timestamp. Cleanup runs on a schedule, not at the exact threshold. Stored account match statistics are preserved. Unsettled results or invalid timestamps and ownership references may require separate review before deletion.

Request deletion under Profile > Account > Delete account or via support@knanzogames.se. After your identity has been checked, deletion is processed in stages. It covers your profile, username reservation, individual game sessions, friend connections, invitations and notification data. The sign-in account is removed after the data cleanup. A pending or in-progress status does not mean deletion is complete.

Shared match and group results may be retained with replacement player identities to preserve other players' results. The context may still make a participant recognisable. An access-restricted record containing the former account ID and deletion status prevents delayed requests from recreating the account. These records currently have no automatic expiry and are not guaranteed to be anonymous.

Support enquiries, logs and any backups are handled separately. Retention depends on resolving the enquiry, security, legal claims and the provider's handling. There is not yet a common scheduled deletion process for all this data. Contact us about remaining data or to request deletion. We must not retain data for longer than its purpose requires.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account. We do not delete your Apple or Google account. Any app-store subscription must be cancelled separately.

9. Your rights

You can request access and a copy, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing. Subject to the GDPR's conditions, data you have provided can also be transferred in a machine-readable format (data portability). Applicable exceptions and other people's rights may limit these rights.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests; we must then assess whether there are sufficient grounds to continue. You can always object to direct marketing. Consent can be withdrawn without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Email support@knanzogames.se. Proportionate identity verification may be needed. We normally respond within one month. If the complexity or number of requests justifies an extension of up to two months, we will inform you during the first month. Requests are normally free of charge. You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) at imy.se without contacting us first.

Scores and game rules are calculated automatically. Contact us if you want to challenge an account or security decision. The GDPR provides specific protection against solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects; ordinary score calculation is not in itself such a decision.

10. Younger players and changes

Ziffer is intended for people aged 13 and over. The age limit does not entitle you to enter into every contract or make purchases on your own. Ask a parent or guardian for help when needed. You or your parent or guardian can contact us if someone under 13 has created an account, in cases of harassment or for help with your data.

The current policy is available in the app and on the website. We will explain material changes before new processing begins and request fresh consent where required. Updated information does not automatically give us the right to use data for new purposes.
