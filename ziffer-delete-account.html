Delete in the app

Open Profile, Account, Delete account (Profil, Konto, Radera konto in Swedish). Read the confirmation and verify your sign-in when requested. Once the server accepts the request, further account-related changes are blocked and cleanup proceeds in stages. The sign-in account is removed after data cleanup.

An accepted request or a pending or processing status does not mean deletion is complete. Contact support about errors or unclear status.

Contact us without the app

Email support@knanzogames.se with the subject Ziffer account deletion. Include your username or account email if available. Contact: Knanzo Games, Plangatan 2, 441 70 Sollebrunn, Sweden; telephone +46763214684. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Never send passwords, sign-in codes or card details. We may need proportionate information to verify account ownership or a representative's authority. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested through a secure channel. An email does not automatically execute deletion.

Records covered by cleanup

The deletion flow covers private and public player profiles, username reservations, account-linked individual game sessions and competition entries, friend links, invitations and notification records. Account-linked rewarded-video sessions and counters are also included. This describes the cleanup's scope, not confirmation that your account has already been deleted. Other players' accounts are not deleted.

Records that may remain

Shared match and group records may retain scores, categories and context with replacement player identities. This is not guaranteed anonymisation: other players may recognise the context.

A restricted record tied to your former account ID remains to prevent delayed requests from recreating data. It contains deletion status, timestamps and limited sign-in provider revocation information. It has no automatic expiry.

Some reward receipts, provider logs, backups and support correspondence may also remain. We cannot currently give confirmed retention periods for these records or the blocking record. Account deletion therefore does not immediately erase every copy. You may request information about remaining data and the reasons for retaining it.

Responses and your rights

GDPR requests must be handled without undue delay, normally with a response within one month. If complexity or the number of requests justifies a lawful extension of up to two further months, you must receive notice and reasons within the first month. This is not a promise of a particular technical completion time.

If we refuse a request, we must explain why and how to complain. You can also request access or correction without deleting your account. Read about erasure and complaints to Sweden's privacy authority, IMY: https://www.imy.se/privatperson/dataskydd/dina-rattigheter/radering/.

Sign-in providers and subscriptions

Deleting Ziffer does not delete your Apple or Google account. Ziffer's deletion confirmation does not confirm removal of provider-held data or sign-in permissions.

Purchases and restoration are currently disabled. Any store subscription must be cancelled separately in App Store or Google Play; account deletion and uninstalling do not stop billing.
